Calculating site scores…

Scores are comparative screening indicators, not probabilities of mission success. Terrain openness uses the fraction of azimuth bins with horizon angles ≤ 5°. Results depend on the selected evaluation period. The overall score is a weighted screening metric combining sunlight availability (40%), geometric Earth visibility (30%), and terrain openness (30%). Individual metrics are normalized to a 0–100 scale. Scores support relative comparison between candidate sites and do not represent mission safety probabilities or final landing-site suitability.

i
Preliminary analysis notice

Ephemeris positions are from JPL Horizons and terrain obstruction is derived from the supplied DEMs. Results remain preliminary until the DEM horizontal orientation, vertical datum, observer-point selection, and horizon calculation are independently validated. Earth visibility is geometric line of sight, not proof of an operational communications link.
